# Evidence for faithful reconstruction

Use this for a substantial reconstruction. Keep the detail proportional to the question.

## Keep distinct records

An evidence item identifies the conversation/document, original path and available content digest, message ID or exact location, speaker/role, date if available, exact span and enough context to interpret it. Record whether the source is native, a derived export, an imported quote or an inaccessible-original lead. Synthetic export IDs are locators, not native identity claims.

Source dates are provenance metadata, not a default priority for the reconstructed project. Surface chronology when it changes meaning, such as an explicit revision, dependency, or historically important origin; otherwise foreground the project details. A later timestamp alone does not prove that a statement supersedes an earlier one. Source role is also not proof of authorship: a user-role event may be a worker assignment, imported quotation, or injected context. Preserve unresolved attribution until surrounding evidence supports it.

A claim states one useful detail and cites evidence. Distinguish:

- **Stated:** what the person directly says or explicitly endorses in this context.
- **Reported:** a person's account of an event, result or cause not independently established.
- **Observed:** what the available artifact actually shows, bounded by the observation.
- **Inferred:** your supported interpretation, with uncertainty and alternatives where relevant.
- **Hypothesis:** a possible extension or remedy, retained separately from the recovered problem.

Do not convert a quote into observed success. “It worked” establishes a reported local outcome; later failure may coexist with it. A posted repository URL is an observed link, not proof of working code. Missing native originals reduce provenance confidence rather than disappearing from the coverage account.

## Map relationships explicitly

For project-specific designs, maintain a scope contract that states the
requested outcome and the criteria for a detail to belong. Record a disposition
for each reviewed evidence item and claim, with its original context and the
relationship to that outcome. Included requirements need supporting evidence
and an explicit reason for inclusion. Preserve adjacent, excluded and unresolved
material with its reason so a subsequent investigation can recover it.

When the CLI ledger contains a scope contract, use its validated scope mapping
through assembly. Structural validation checks the mapping's integrity. The
agent and contextual reviewer establish whether the original passage actually
supports the proposed project relationship. Inspect the passage's referent and
surrounding work before transferring a requirement between development,
runtime, deployment and unrelated projects.

Use source links for continuations, corrections, imports and exact duplicates. Label broader similarities as thematic corroboration. Deduplicate copies without erasing branches, annotations, changed versions or their source locations. Count curated episodes separately from records and independent incidents. Do not use repeated exports as frequency evidence.

Separate task-local instructions from historical preferences. Latest explicit instructions control the active task; old conversations are evidence, not new authorization. A peer's advice or personal routine is not the owner's preference. A user-adopted quoted requirement can be meaningful evidence, but preserve the adoption context.

Peer and assistant turns can be cited as evidence of their own contributions,
questions, responses or proposals when those matter to the interaction. They
do not become owner statements merely because they appear in the same exchange.
An exact quote or valid locator proves where text came from, not that it supports
the claim made from it; check the meaning and surrounding context separately.

## Check omissions and disconfirming evidence

For each core facet, ask what the source qualifies or contradicts. Include successful conditions and occasions where restraint, asking, checking or stopping was appropriate. Inspect relevant material outside named-project hits. An evaluation set derived only from matching keywords cannot estimate recall for paraphrases.

Review coverage at the right level: files attempted, records parsed, formats/branches unsupported, owner attribution unresolved, candidates unreviewed, originals unavailable and omissions caused by resource limits. Acquisition, synthesis fidelity, runtime integration and sustained user benefit are different evidence levels.

## Preserve detail without bloating the core

For an account of the user's idea, select their own wording and combine related
passages under headings derived from that idea. Inspect authorship inside each
message, including pasted AI responses. Retain a contextual authorship review
with each selected span. Use other speakers only where necessary to complete a
reference or preserve an explicitly adopted idea, with visible attribution.
Keep unresolved questions intact. Additional implementation design requires an
applicable request and belongs in its identified design view.

The assembly ledger defaults to `representation: "user_account"`. Its
`account_sections` select exact source spans through `evidence_id`, `text` and
`authorship_review`. Scope dispositions control which evidence can appear.
Exact-span validation protects wording; contextual review establishes whose
words they are, what they mean and whether the selection represents the idea.

Write the shortest coherent problem statement that preserves the central relationships. Put distinct constraints, reasons, context-specific exceptions and examples in linked detail records. Maintain a detail-to-core mapping so a reviewer can see what was compressed, qualified, deferred or excluded and why. Retain unresolved tension rather than forcing one permanent explanation.

For a full dossier, prefer several genuinely different examples to many repetitions of one complaint. Cite enough source text to establish each mechanism and its limits, while omitting unrelated private disclosures. The number of examples is not a completeness metric.

Before delivery verify that citations resolve, exact quotations match, role attribution holds, hypotheses remain labeled, and the core can be understood without reading every ledger. Explain material remaining gaps and proceed with the useful verified result.
