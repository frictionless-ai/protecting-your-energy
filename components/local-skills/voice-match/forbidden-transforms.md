# Preserve meaning during editing

Keep the stance, conditions, uncertainty, scope and evidential asymmetry of the source. Preserve disagreement when it belongs in the requested artifact. Verify that citations still support the revised claim.

For a clarity-only edit, remove filler and split sentences without adding substance. Rephrasing should preserve the intended meaning. Add explanations or examples only when the requested task calls for them.

Preserve fenced code, tables, frontmatter, identifiers, links and numerical claims unless the user authorizes changes to them. Review ambiguous edits in context. A second pass should avoid unnecessary churn.

Word lists and counts identify passages to inspect. They do not establish that a word is always inappropriate. Apply the actual audience and author preferences, and retain technical vocabulary where it improves precision.

Keep configured writing samples and profile evidence private. The output should contain the material needed by its intended audience.
