# Additional local workflow skills

Ten portable local variants preserve planning, secret handling, markdown guards, idea packaging, triage, background improvement, durable capture, evidence review, sponsor access and catalog lookup.

The authoritative source copies remain outside this bundle. Sponsorware identifies its upstream as [bodencrouch/sponsorware-skill](https://github.com/bodencrouch/sponsorware-skill) and retains its MIT notice. Original authorship of the other nine source copies remains unestablished. Local variants are identified by their source lineage and adaptations.

Private identity defaults, conversation examples, training corpus and evolution logs remain external. The secret helpers use private configurable storage and contain no account identities. The evidence-review method is generalized from the local operative instructions. Its historical engagement measurements and private exemplars are omitted.

The markdown guard retains its runtime modules and verdict reference. Existing source tests remain at the original location. The catalog helper uses the external `SKILL_LIBRARY` directory and standard-library Python. Its enable and disable operations apply only when authorized.

These imports have received inline Python AST and shell syntax checks. Secret operations, publication checks and host registration require their actual task context. The sponsorware helper supplies limited historical checks whose scope and skipped operations must be evaluated before a release.
