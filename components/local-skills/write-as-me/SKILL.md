# Write as me

Recover what the author wants to communicate, the recipient, relevant source facts and the intended effect. Use the current conversation and authorized examples before asking for recoverable context.

`PYE_VOICE_PROFILE_DIR` can identify a private `voice-bank.md`, `voice-spec.md`, register guidance and evolution notes. The [voice-bank template](voice-bank.example.md) shows the fields a private configuration can contain. Authentic examples and later corrections must retain their source and scope.

Draft the requested artifact directly. Lead with the substantive point when the audience needs it. Explain mechanisms and preserve the author’s uncertainty, reasoning and disagreement. Keep the degree of formality appropriate to the recipient.

Use evidence the reader can assess for factual claims. Distinguish a source-backed statement from a proposed extension. Avoid introducing a personal assertion merely to imitate style.

Apply plain-ui-copy to short UI labels and voice-match to existing prose when those workflows fit. A reply should answer the present conversational move and retain the author’s purpose. Verify that the final wording preserves the source meaning and the requested format.

Comments should explain consequential decisions where helpful. Commit messages should describe the actual change and preserve required attribution.

Private voice evolution follows the user’s authorization and the host’s memory rules. A drafting task can proceed using the available context while an optional profile improvement remains deferred.

Repository-history modification and external sending retain their own authorization. Deliver the requested draft without inventing those actions.
