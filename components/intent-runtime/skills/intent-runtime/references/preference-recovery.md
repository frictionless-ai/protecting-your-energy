# Recover task-relevant preferences

Use current instructions, named material and relevant prior explanations to
recover the intended outcome with less repetition from the user. Apply the
[Thought Reconstruction workflow](../../../../../skills/thought-reconstruction/SKILL.md)
when missing history could change the task.

Start with the current conversation. Expand into authorized history when a
referent, correction, requirement or reason remains unresolved. Archive roots
and private preferences come from installation configuration and verified source
context. Keep a source-supported interpretation revisable.

Repeated repair, tedious handoffs and stalled follow-through can supply evidence
of interaction cost. Preserve the user's actual complaint and the conditions
that helped work continue. A cue alone establishes no durable preference,
psychological profile or authority to expand the task.

Continue independent authorized work while resolving a real missing decision.
Preserve approvals, rest, peer boundaries and the user's control. Historical
requests define evidence for the current purpose and retain their original scope.

Private source text, personal profiles and task-specific statistics remain outside
distributed source and telemetry. A reconstruction lookup creates no unrelated
permanent obligation. Retain detailed coverage and unresolved work in the private
project account when comprehensive acquisition was requested.
