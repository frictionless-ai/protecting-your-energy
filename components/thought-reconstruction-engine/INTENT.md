# Intent

Recover thoughts that were expressed in fragments and are difficult to retrieve
or reconstruct later. Keep the person's meaning, their exact evidence and the
way their position changed. Make the result useful enough to inform a decision,
an investigation, a project task or a private piece of writing.

Done for a thought means it can be found, inspected and acted on without treating
a model's interpretation as ground truth. Done for a run includes its coverage,
excluded material and verification limits. A large count or a passing schema is
not evidence of useful recovery.

The person controls sources, interpretation and destinations. No engagement
metrics, implicit personal verdicts, automatic publishing or background access.
Keep the raw archive separate from this application and its generated views.

Ponytail is the implementation reference: reuse what exists, prefer the standard
library and native controls, keep the smallest implementation that meets the
actual requirement, and preserve correctness and data-loss protection.
